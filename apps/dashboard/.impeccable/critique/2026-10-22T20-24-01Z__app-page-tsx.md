## Design Specificity Verdict

The Spanish store context, Makced branding, and featured-product section give the page some product fit. However, the mint-accented sidebar, four KPI cards, and generic summary layout could belong to many ecommerce admin tools. It reads as a tidy inventory overview, not yet a distinctive Makced store-operations surface.

## Overall Impression

The page is readable and approachable, but its current job is mostly to report totals. Its biggest opportunity is to clarify what store operators should do next, then make the summary and the rest of the dashboard support that task consistently.

## What's Working

- Clear Spanish labels and a recognizable hierarchy from greeting to metrics to featured products.
- Responsive card grids and mobile bottom navigation are already implemented.
- The empty featured-products state explains the next step and links directly to the catalog.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Sidebar/content overlap at 481–640px.** The main content switches to a 68px offset below 1024px, but the sidebar remains 220px until 640px and only becomes bottom navigation at 480px or below. At 481–640px, the fixed sidebar can cover content. Align the sidebar and content breakpoints and verify tablet widths. (`app/globals.css`; `components/sidebar.css`)
2. **[P2] KPI cards look interactive but are not.** They lift on hover but are non-link `<div>` elements. Users can reasonably expect a click to open the relevant workflow. Either make them keyboard-accessible shortcuts or remove the interactive hover affordance. (`app/page.css`; `app/page.tsx`)
3. **[P2] Counts lack decision context.** Raw totals have no period, change, or freshness cue, so the dashboard provides limited operational guidance. Add context only where reliable data exists; do not imply unimplemented sales or live-health metrics. (`app/page.tsx`; `actions/resumen.ts`)
4. **[P2] Product ranking cues are ambiguous.** Numbered rows look like ranking, while the query sorts featured products by creation date. Product images are selected but not displayed. Clarify the sort basis and either use or remove the unused image data. (`actions/resumen.ts`; `app/page.tsx`; `app/page.css`)

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** KPI cards do not route directly to related workflows; no visible shortcuts are offered.
- **Sam (accessibility-dependent):** Tablet navigation hides visual text labels, forcing recognition of icons or tooltips.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** Metrics have labels but no contextual help explaining what counts mean or what action to take.

## Minor Observations

- Prices use `$` with Colombian number formatting but no explicit currency code.
- The animated “Tienda activa” badge reads like live health status, but the page does not show a corresponding health signal.
- Five primary navigation choices exceed the four-item cognitive-load guideline; they are still a manageable set, but should be watched as the dashboard grows.

## Questions to Consider

- Should KPI cards stay informational, become shortcuts to workflows, or lead to a detail view?
